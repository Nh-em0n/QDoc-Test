### 📝 Notes on Valency

- **Valency** is the combining capacity of an element — the number of electrons an atom can gain, lose, or share to complete its outermost shell.
- For **metals** (Li, Be, Na, Mg, Al, K, Ca), valency = number of electrons in the outermost shell (they lose them).
- For **nonmetals** (N, O, F, P, S, Cl), valency = 8 − (number of outermost electrons) (they gain or share electrons).
- **Carbon and Silicon** have valency 4 (they share 4 electrons).
- **Noble gases** (He, Ne, Ar) have valency 0 because their outermost shells are full and they do not readily combine.
